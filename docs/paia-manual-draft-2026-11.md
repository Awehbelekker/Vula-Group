## 1. Introduction

PAIA gives everyone the right to ask for records held by a private body, where those records are
needed to use or protect a right. This manual explains:

- who we are and how to contact our Information Officer;
- what records we hold;
- how we process personal information under POPIA;
- how to ask for a record, and what it costs.

[Company legal name] ("Vula", "we", "us") runs Vula: an AI business assistant that small businesses
use on WhatsApp and in a web dashboard. Businesses use it for orders, invoices, documents, projects
and customer conversations.

Vula holds two kinds of personal information:

- **Its own records**, for which it is the **responsible party** (for example the accounts of
  businesses that use Vula, and their owners and staff).
- **Records it processes for businesses that use Vula**, as their **operator** (for example those
  businesses' customers' messages, orders and invoices). For these records, **the business is the
  responsible party.** See section 9.

## 7. Processing of personal information (POPIA, via PAIA s51(1)(c))

### 7.1 Why we process personal information

- To provide the Vula service to businesses: answering questions from their own records, taking
  orders, sending quotes and invoices, recording payments, keeping books, and sending the messages
  they ask for.
- To manage accounts of businesses that use Vula, including logins, access, billing and AI usage.
- To research prices and suppliers on the web when a team member asks.
- To keep the service secure, find and fix faults, and check that answers are correct.
- To meet legal duties, including keeping accounting records.
- To respond to enquiries made through our website.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use any business's information to train AI models.

### 7.2 Categories of data subjects and their information

| Data subjects | Personal information |
|---|---|
| Owners and staff of businesses using Vula | Name, phone number, email, login details, two-step sign-in link, role and access, their own chats with Vula, private notes they ask Vula to keep, research notes, chat attachments (kept 1 day) |
| Customers, clients and contacts of those businesses | WhatsApp number and name, messages (including voice notes, photos and documents), orders, invoices, payments, bookings, contact details, a short note about earlier conversations (if the business uses it), property enquiries and buyer preferences (for property businesses) |
| People whose details appear in documents or mail a business files with Vula | Whatever the document or email contains (for example names and contact details on invoices). Mail in folders named as personal (such as Personal, Private, Medical, Family) is never read |
| People who enquire through Vula's website | Name and contact details given in the enquiry |
| Vula's suppliers and service providers | Contact and contract details |
| Vula's staff and contractors | [confirm] |

**Special personal information.** If a message suggests someone may harm themselves, Vula replies
with crisis helpline numbers and tells one person at the business, with the number but never the
message. No content is logged.

### 7.5 Security measures (in general terms)

- Each business's records are kept apart, enforced by the database on every table.
- Access by role; removing a team member removes their login.
- Two-step sign-in available for every login.
- Encryption in transit and at rest; saved passwords and keys encrypted again with a separate,
  changeable key.
- Digital signatures checked on incoming messages from WhatsApp and payment and app providers.
- Changes that send money or messages, or change records, are confirmed by a person and checked after.
- Daily backups, kept for 7 days.
- Automated tests on every change and regular audits.
- Personal information is deleted automatically after set periods (see the privacy policy, section 8).

## 8. How to request access to a record (PAIA)

1. **Use the prescribed form.** Complete Form 2 of the PAIA Regulations (request for access to a
   record) [verify form number]. It is available from the Regulator's website and from our Information
   Officer.
2. **Send it to the Information Officer** at the email or address in section 2.
3. **Give enough detail** for us to find the record, say how you want access (for example a copy or
   inspection), and explain which right you want to use or protect.
4. **Prove who you are.** If you ask on behalf of someone else, show that you may act for them.
5. **Pay the request fee** if one applies (section 10).

We will decide within 30 days of receiving the request. We may extend this once, by up to 30 days,
and will tell you why. If we do not reply in time, the request is treated as refused. [verify: PAIA
s56–s58]

We may refuse access only on the grounds in Part 3, Chapter 4 of PAIA (for example to protect another
person's privacy, commercial information of a third party, or confidential information). We will give
reasons.

If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Information Regulator or apply to court [verify:
PAIA s77A and s78, and time limits].

## 9. Requests about a business's customers' information

Vula holds much personal information **as an operator** for businesses that use it. For those
records, the business is the responsible party.

- If you are a customer of such a business, send your request to that business first. We will help it
  answer you.
- You can also use these words on the business's WhatsApp number: **EXPORT** (get a copy of your
  conversations and your own orders and invoices, emailed to the address on file [confirm: needs the
  email service switched on]), **DELETE** (delete your conversations and records with that business
  straight away), **STOP** (no more marketing) and **START** (turn it back on).
- Orders, invoices, payments, bookings, sales pipeline records and emails a business sent you from a
  project page are kept, because they are the business's financial and business records.
