# Pre-launch audit, 17 Nov 2026: the 26 changes since 13 Nov

Base: `master` at `28c546d` (#339). This audit covers #314–#339, about 13,600 lines added and
never audited before. It follows the 13-point audit of 13 Nov (`docs/audit-2026-11-13.md`).

**What came in.**
- Ask anything / open chat, with Confirm and Cancel in the dashboard chat (#314).
- Chat attachments and search over earlier chats (#315).
- Research mode and research notes reused by the team (#316–#318, #330).
- Spreadsheets out of the chat (#319).
- AI allowance and top-ups (#320).
- Trade costing phases 1–4: market prices, build-ups, estimates, actuals (#322–#326, #328–#331, #337).
- Claude Sonnet 5 as the cloud model under zero retention (#332), and answer checks (#333, #334, #337–#339).
- Download requests sent to every decider (#335).
- Emailing a project's client from the business's mailbox (#336).

**Method.** Production was read only. Four reviewers each read one area of the diff end to end:
- isolation and access;
- money, outward messages and AI actions;
- privacy and data flows;
- hardening, untrusted input and the frontend.

Each finding was re-read in the code before it went into a fix. The fixes are in three commits on
one PR, each with tests that fail on the old code: 10 for access, 13 for money and 15 for privacy.

## Result

No business can read another business's records. Every new table has RLS (236 of 236 in
production), with no policy for the anon or authenticated keys.

None of the findings was HIGH. There were eight MEDIUM findings, all fixed, and eleven LOW, of
which eight are fixed. Of the three LOW ones still open, the owner decided two on 18 Nov to keep things
as they are, and one code item remains, listed under "Still open".

## Fixed

### Access
- **Costing by trade was open to every team member** (MED). Someone with only the Orders tab could
  list estimates and see what each job actually cost: supplier, unit price and worker pay rates.
  - Estimates now need Money access or the Costing by trade tab.
  - What a job actually cost is for owners and managers only, as `compare_estimate` already was on WhatsApp.
  - `estimate_cost` on WhatsApp now needs the Costing by trade tab.
- **Build-ups could be confirmed without a preview** (MED). The model could confirm a researched
  build-up on its first call. It now has to show it first.
- **The dashboard Confirm** ran with full access and no name (LOW). It now runs with the signed-in
  person's own access and identity.
- **A caller with no login could name a person's own thread** (LOW). Session ids of the form `m:` and `u:` are now refused.

### Money and costing
- **Formula injection in "Download as spreadsheet"** (MED). A web page title or table cell beginning
  with `=` became a live formula, which could send the sheet's contents to an outside site when opened.
  - Every text cell is now written as text, and so is the estimate's Unit column.
  - Source links can only be web links.
- **A web page could replace a shared market price** (MED). Prices are shared by every business.
  Any page that ranked for "cement 50kg price" could overwrite Cashbuild's row.
  - A web-researched price is now kept only where Vula has a plausible band for it.
  - It is kept under its own site's name, beside the known supplier's price rather than over it.
- **No limit on costing research** (MED). One estimate with 300 lines meant hundreds of paid
  searches and cloud calls, outside the AI allowance. Now:
  - at most 40 items per estimate, with 3 new build-ups researched and 12 price searches per estimate;
  - the research endpoints have the same per-person hourly limit as research in the chat;
  - both task types stop when the allowance is used up.
- **The usage meter could lose calls** (MED). Two calls finishing together overwrote each other's
  count, so AI use went unbilled. Each call is now added in one statement (migration 289); the old
  path runs until the migration is applied.
- **A top-up could be both cancelled and marked paid** (LOW). The change now only applies while it is still requested.

### Privacy
- **Research notes carried the asker's private thread to colleagues** (MED, found by two reviewers).
  - An answer written with the person's own thread or private notes in front of it is now kept for them only. They can still share it themselves.
  - A colleague is never shown the question as it was asked.
- **Questions went to the search provider word for word** (MED). Every search is now scrubbed before
  it leaves Vula: emails, phone, account and ID numbers, rand amounts and titled names are removed.
  This covers research, the web_search skill and the fallback query. Street addresses and untitled
  names can still get through, so the search provider is listed as a sub-processor.
- **Chat attachments were not removed after their day** (LOW). They are now:
  - swept daily for every business (a retention rule);
  - deleted along with a team member's data;
  - refused when oversized without being read whole into memory.
- **Expired research notes left their question in the index** (LOW). The index entry now goes too.
- **The document-analysis escalation was logged under the wrong model** (LOW). It is now logged as its own cloud decision, naming the model.

## Checked and sound

- **The dashboard Confirm (#314):** it runs the stored, previewed arguments, works once only, expires, and is tied to the person's own thread.
- **Search over earlier chats:** it only searches the caller's own threads.
- **Attachments:** each is tied to the person who attached it and is never written into the history.
- **Zero retention on cloud calls:** every new call goes through the wrapper that asks for zero retention, including research, open chat, build-ups, rate extraction and answer checks. No call bypasses it.
- **Cloud model availability under zero retention:** `ai_health` checks every 6 hours that each cloud model is reachable under zero retention, so a gap would show.
- **Fetching web pages for research:** this goes through the safe fetcher, which blocks private addresses and cloud metadata addresses, checks every redirect, and caps size and time.
- **AI allowance:** amounts are in whole cents and worked out on the server. Only the master can mark a top-up paid.
- **Client email (#336):**
  - owner and managers only;
  - the owner writes it and confirms it in the dashboard;
  - recorded and read back;
  - no header injection.
- **Download requests (#335):**
  - only the business's deciders are asked;
  - one request per document, and at most 20 open per link;
  - outside WhatsApp's 24-hour window the approved template is used.
- **Trade costing arithmetic:** it uses Decimal and whole cents, and the model never adds anything up. The VAT basis is handled consistently.
- **Dashboard:** no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, and links are web links only. No secrets in the frontend and no new dependencies.

## Still open

### Decided by the owner (18 Nov)
1. **Build-ups stay shared with every Vula business**, whatever its network-sharing switch says. What's shared is quantities only, with no file names, notes, prices or who confirmed it. The client statement says so.
2. **The client-email record** (`vula_project_client_emails`) is a business record, kept like an invoice. A client's delete request doesn't remove it. The client statement says so.

### In the code (next)
- Research notes from a login with no team row, or from an API key, can't be found by delete or export.
- Recipients of a client email aren't checked against the project's own client contacts.
- A research answer that a web page manipulated is still kept and reused for teammates. The untrusted-content fences don't neutralise a closing marker written inside the page text.
- Carried over from 13 Nov:
  - rate limits keyed on `X-Forwarded-For`;
  - the enquiry-form cap;
  - Smart Scanner grounding;
  - retention for more tables;
  - CSP and HSTS;
  - dependency scanning in CI;
  - storefront Next.js.
