### Objection Handling (quick responses)

**"We already use ChatGPT"**
> ChatGPT is general AI — it doesn't know your pricing, your clients, or how your business writes a quote. CogniCore learns your specific business. Very different thing. Worth seeing the demo.

**"It sounds complicated to set up"**
> It takes about an hour. You upload a few of your existing documents — old quotes, your price list, some SOPs — and the AI learns from them. We handle everything else. You're using it on day one.

**"What about our data security?"**
> Everything stays in South Africa. POPIA compliant. Your data is never used to train anyone else's AI. We can give you the full technical breakdown — it's genuinely one of our strongest selling points vs US-based tools.

**"We can't afford it right now"**
> The free trial is genuinely free — no card, nothing. Try it for 30 days. If it doesn't save you more time than it costs, cancel. Most clients make back the monthly fee in the first week of saved admin time.

**"We have IT/a system already"**
> CogniCore isn't an IT system — it's an AI layer on top of how your team already works. It connects to your email, runs on WhatsApp, and fits into what you're already doing. No replacement required.

### The Demo Script (15 minutes)

**Minutes 0–3: The problem**
"Tell me — what's the thing your team spends the most time on that feels like it shouldn't take that long?"
(Let them answer. Most common: quotes, follow-ups, admin, training new staff.)

**Minutes 3–8: Live demo**
Open CogniCore on your phone/laptop. Type:
> "Draft a quote for [their company] for [whatever service they mentioned]"

Show it producing a real quote in their format with their pricing.

Then: "Now ask it something about your own company."

**Minutes 8–12: The fit question**
"Which of your team members spends the most time on [the thing they mentioned]? What would it mean for them if this handled 80% of that?"

**Minutes 12–15: Trial close**
"The setup takes about an hour. We upload your price list and a couple of quotes you've done before, and you're live. Want to do that this week? First 30 days are free — you won't pay anything until you decide it's worth keeping."
