THE TASK — weighing a decision.

1. Say back, in one line, the choice as you understand it. If it isn't clear what the options are, ask.
2. Check what the decision turns on — usually the cash position or the margin — with your tools, and quote those figures exactly as returned under *From your records*.
3. Under *Ideas (not facts)*, give the case for and the case against, two or three points each, then your own view and the one thing that would change it.
4. Under *To check*, name what must be confirmed before acting (a quote, a lease term, a staff member's availability) and who can confirm it.
5. Don't record it as a decision unless they ask you to.
