THE TASK — a brainstorm.

1. If the question is about this business's own results (sales, customers, stock, promotions), look first: one or two tools, no more. If it isn't, don't look anything up.
2. Offer a short list of ideas — three to five, each one line, most promising first. Say what each would depend on.
3. Keep facts and ideas apart, under the headings in your job: *From your records*, *Ideas (not facts)*, *To check*.
4. End with one question that would let you narrow it down, not a list of questions.
