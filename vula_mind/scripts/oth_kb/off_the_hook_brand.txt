ABOUT OFF THE HOOK
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Off the Hook is a Cape Town-based fresh fish and pasture-raised chicken delivery business.
WhatsApp: 073 781 5979 | Email: info@offthehook.capetown | Website: offthehook.co.za

Founders:
- Stacy — Owner and founder. Stacy started Off the Hook because she was tired of not
  knowing where her fish came from. She sources directly from Cape Town fishing communities
  and small-scale fishermen, keeping supply chains short and quality high.
- Roland — Operations. Roland runs the day-to-day: supplier relationships, delivery
  logistics, stock management, and making sure every order goes out right.

Mission: Quality food delivered to your door — you know exactly where it came from.

WHAT MAKES OFF THE HOOK DIFFERENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. DIRECT SOURCING
   Fish comes straight from Cape Town's fishing communities — no middlemen.
   Stacy has personal relationships with fishermen along the West and South coasts.
   This means fresher fish, fair prices for fishermen, and full traceability.

2. CATCH OF THE DAY
   The fresh fish range rotates weekly based on what's actually been caught.
   Popular species include: yellowtail, snoek, kabeljou (kob), yellowfin tuna,
   jacopever (Cape soldier), hake, kingklip. Availability changes with seasons and
   weather — WhatsApp to ask what's fresh this week.

3. PASTURE-RAISED CHICKEN
   Off the Hook's chicken is GMO-free, hormone-free, and antibiotic-free.
   The birds are raised on pasture — not in cages. No brine added.
   Available fresh (whole, portions, deboned) and frozen.

4. FROZEN SEAFOOD & EXTRAS
   Quality frozen options for those who want to stock up: Norwegian salmon, prawns,
   calamari, mussels, fish cakes. Also bone broth made from the chicken carcasses.

ORDERING & DELIVERY

How to order:
  1. WhatsApp 073 781 5979 — type "hi" to get the menu, or browse offthehook.co.za
  2. Add items to your cart via the AI assistant or the website shop
  3. Checkout via Yoco secure payment link — card, tap, EFT accepted

Delivery:
  - Cape Town delivery only (currently)
  - Delivery fee: R80 per order
  - Morning slot: before 12:00 | Afternoon slot: 12:00–18:00 | Express available
  - Orders placed before 10:00 usually qualify for same-day afternoon delivery
  - Next-day morning delivery for orders placed after 10:00

Cut-off times:
  - Fresh fish and chicken is ordered from suppliers daily based on demand
  - Frozen and extras are available for same-day dispatch until 14:00
  - WhatsApp Stacy or Roland directly for urgent or large orders

Payment: Yoco (card/tap/QR), EFT on request for large orders.

PRODUCT GUIDE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FRESH FISH (per kg, weekly rotation):
- Yellowfin Tuna: R290/kg — steaks, sushi, poke, sear rare. Rich and firm.
- Kingklip: R290/kg fillets, R240/kg centre cuts — Cape Town's premium fish. Sweet, no bones.
- Hake: R220/kg centre cuts, R160/kg fillets — the classic. Bake, fry, braai.
- Kabeljou (Kob): ask — firm white flesh, great for potjie or whole on the braai.
- Snoek: seasonal — the Cape staple. Braaied with apricot jam or smoked.
- Yellowtail: seasonal — firm, versatile, excellent on the braai.
- Jacopever: R75/kg — great value, firm, mild. Perfect for curries or fish cakes.
- Octopus: R95/kg (whole, unclean) — braai, slow cook, or potjie.

FRESH PASTURE-RAISED CHICKEN (per kg or pack):
- Whole chicken: R95 each — no hormones, no brine
- Breast fillets: R150/pack (4–5 per pack)
- Deboned thighs: R185/kg — juicy, versatile, great for braai or curries
- Drumsticks: R150/pack (6 per pack)
- Spatchcock: R150 frozen — great for quick braai
- Livers, feet, necks, carcasses — for the whole-animal cook

EXTRAS:

Stacy and Roland update the "daily catch" highlights each morning based on what
came in fresh. The Vula AI assistant will show these automatically when you say hi.

Typical catch of the day items rotate between:
- Cape yellowtail (summer: Jan–Apr, Aug–Oct)
- Snoek (winter: May–Sep)
- Yellowfin tuna (year-round when available)
- Kingklip (year-round, premium)
- Kabeljou / Kob (year-round)
- Wild-caught linefish of the week (varies)

To mark a product as "catch of the day", Stacy or Roland updates it in the
Vula admin dashboard — the AI picks it up immediately.

FAQ
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: Is the fish fresh or frozen?
A: Fresh fish is caught and delivered same/next day. We also carry a range of quality frozen seafood.

Q: Do you deliver outside Cape Town?
A: Cape Town only for now — we're working on expanding.

Q: Can I order for a function or large group?
A: Yes — WhatsApp Stacy directly for bulk orders. She can arrange specific catch, cut sizes, and quantities.

Q: Is your chicken really hormone and antibiotic free?
A: Yes. Off the Hook's supplier raises birds on open pasture, GMO-free feed, no hormones, no antibiotics, no brine added after processing.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: Yoco (card, tap, QR code) and EFT on request. Secure payment link sent via WhatsApp after ordering.

Q: What if I'm not happy with my order?
A: Contact Stacy directly on WhatsApp — she'll sort it out, no questions asked.
